# hob mark

The orange household-spirit mark is original generated artwork for hob. Its
silhouette suggests a lowercase h and a helpful spirit who handles chores while
you sleep. The transparent PNG is bundled locally for the app, default avatar,
sign-in screen, browser icon, and project README.

Created with the built-in imagegen tool, transparent-background mode, on
2026-10-10. Final prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create a finished original app icon/symbol for hob, a
> personal agent named after the helpful household spirits who do chores while
> you sleep. One single compact memorable symbol only, no text or wordmark. A
> friendly abstract little night spirit whose silhouette subtly suggests a
> lowercase h: two softly rounded upright forms joined by a smooth arch, one tiny
> warm spark/ear, and two simple dark eye cutouts. Premium minimal geometric mark,
> vector-friendly solid shapes with slightly organic corners, quietly magical
> rather than cartoonish. Warm amber/coral-orange symbol that stays readable on
> both charcoal and light ivory surfaces. Flat colors, restrained two-color
> maximum; no gradients, no shadows, no metallic or 3D rendering, no surrounding
> badge, no frame, no mockup, no extra scattered decorations. Centered large with
> a modest transparent margin on all sides. Truly transparent background. The
> result must remain recognizable at 24 pixels.
